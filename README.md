## Project Overview
The purpose of this data analysis is to understand specific descriptive statistics and their relationships based on the “pennstate2” dataset of 200 students in a statistics class for social and behavioral sciences. Stratified analysis is conducted and graphs and tables are created and analyzed to examine the data.

## Variables
The five variables that were examined were gender, whether or not the students has a tattoo, an estimate of the number of CDs the student owns, height, and the number of ear piercings the student has. 

## Methods
The following statistical and analytical methods were used:

    Descriptive statistics and frequency distributions
    
    Data aggregation and group comparisons
    
    Histograms, boxplots, bar charts, and scatterplots
    
    Random sampling
    
    Confidence interval estimation
    
    Linear trend analysis

## Key Findings
The average height of students was approximately 67.03 inches.
Approximately 85% of students reported having no tattoos.
Students with tattoos had a higher average number of ear piercings compared to students without tattoos.
A weak negative linear trend was observed between student height and the number of ear piercings.
The 95% confidence interval for mean student height, based on a sample of 60 students, was approximately 66.27 to 68.17 inches.
